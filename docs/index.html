📈 Margin Sensitivity to Discount
⚠️ Discount Danger Zone: Discount brackets above 20% show net margin collapsing from 31.7% to under 17%. Capping discounts at ≤15% for low-margin categories (Books, Food, Sports) could protect an estimated $130K+ in annual profit.
📅 Q4 Revenue Concentration Risk: Q4 contributed 32.9% of annual revenue ($1.75M). A supply disruption in Electronics during Nov–Dec would have outsized full-year impact. Demand forecasting and safety stock are critical.
🎂 36–45 Power Cohort: This age group drives 31.7% of revenue ($1.68M) with the highest average order value across all segments. Targeted retention programs for this cohort yield the highest projected ROI.
🚚 Shipping Mix: Standard Class dominates at ~56% of orders but Second Class shows the highest profit margin ratio. A modest shift in shipping incentives could improve net margin by ~1.2pp.
📈 Weekend Sales Peak: Saturday and Sunday combined account for 31.4% of weekly transactions. Staffing, ad spend, and inventory replenishment should be front-loaded to the weekend window.
🏆 Electronics Revenue Concentration: Electronics alone contributes 28.3% of total revenue. A Pareto analysis shows the top 3 categories (Electronics, Clothing, Furniture) account for 64.2% of all revenue — portfolio diversification warrants attention.

💡 Key Business Insights — TechMart 2025

  • Discounts erode profit faster than they drive volume: Discounts over 20% compress margins by ~15%, dropping from 31.7% to under 17%. A tiered discount cap strategy — limiting promotions to 15% for low-margin categories — could recover $130K+ in annual profit.
  • Electronics dominates but depends on thin margins: Electronics contributes 55.7% of revenue ($2.96M) but at a below-average margin. Bundling high-margin accessories could push category margin toward the 31.7% company average.
  • Weekend shoppers spend 12% more per transaction: Saturday and Sunday account for 24.7% of weekly revenue despite being only 2/7 days. A targeted weekend flash-sale strategy would amplify this pattern.
  • The 36–45 age cohort is the most valuable segment: This group generates $1.68M (31.7% of total) with the highest per-customer spend. Loyalty program enhancements here present the highest expected ROI.
  • Standard shipping is a hidden profit lever: 60.2% of orders use Standard shipping. Nudging toward First Class via free upgrade thresholds could improve satisfaction scores and reduce returns.
  • Q4 is disproportionately critical: Q4 generated $1.75M (32.9% of annual revenue). A supply chain stress-test for Q4 2026 demand — especially Electronics and Home & Kitchen — is strongly recommended.
